Drive CLI privacy policy

Drive CLI is a personal OAuth application operated by its owner with rclone on the owner's computer. It is used to back up and organize that owner's Google Drive files.

Google data and purpose

With the owner's permission, rclone accesses Drive file contents and metadata to list, upload, download, check, organize, rename, and delete files as requested by the owner. File-management access does not make Drive files public.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and client configuration are stored locally on the owner's computer. File transfers occur directly between the computer and Google Drive. This static website has no application server, does not receive Drive files or tokens, and does not use Google user data for advertising, sale, or model training. Access to files on Google Drive remains subject to the owner's Google sharing settings.

Retention and control

Backups remain in Google Drive until the owner removes them. Local archives, transfer logs, and credentials remain on the owner's devices until removed. The owner can revoke this application's access in Google Account's third-party connections settings and remove the local rclone credentials.

Website hosting

These informational pages do not add analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages may process ordinary hosting request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

The application's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

For questions about access, the owner can use the developer contact shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
